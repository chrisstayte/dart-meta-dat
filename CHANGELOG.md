## 0.2.0

* Added Android platform support wrapping the Meta Wearables DAT Android SDK (v0.5.0).
* Plugin now supports both iOS and Android platforms.

## 0.1.0

* Initial release.
* Wraps Meta Wearables DAT iOS SDK (v0.5.0).
* Core features:
  * Device discovery and registration via `Wearables` class.
  * Camera permission management.
  * Video streaming via `StreamSession` with configurable codec, resolution, and frame rate.
  * Photo capture support (JPEG and HEIC formats).
  * Device compatibility checking.
* iOS platform support only (Android support planned for future release).
